Honest limits

  • HotSpot 7 with research package defaults; not calibrated against silicon and not chip sign-off.
  • The wire figure is a connectivity-weighted Manhattan center-distance proxy, not routed CPLEX wirelength.
  • Chiplet powers are fixed; no transient workload or leakage feedback.
  • Only the signal-portfolio lane is routed by the signal layer, and only in live-signal runs; every other lane, and every fallback, uses a deterministic local policy.
  • One seed is a demonstration, not a statistical result; the leaderboard is descriptive until n ≥ 10 seeds.
  • Small temperature differences between meshes are not resolved physical superiority.
