## Matching a human package requires a reference contract first

**We can reproduce a specified human-engineered reference, but the current fixtures do not yet define a faithful commercial-hardware reproduction.** There are three distinct goals: reconstruct the geometry, reproduce a published simulation under equivalent inputs, and predict measured physical temperatures under documented conditions. A package photograph or floorplan starts the first; a complete modeling contract supports the second; traceable measurements and uncertainty are needed for the third. A previously validated solver does not automatically validate our package stack, power map, cooling boundary, metric extraction or particular build. ([NASA verification and validation](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/wind/valid/tutorial/overview.html); [TAP-2.5D paper, §§III–IV](https://people.bu.edu/joshi/files/Ma_TAP-2.5D-DATE2021.pdf); [HotSpot 6.0 validation limitations, §2](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/~skadron/Papers/HotSpot60_TR.pdf))

The closest architectural comparison is **TAP-2.5D's reconstruction of the original Ascend910 layout**. The paper's default is a **45 × 45 mm interposer and 64 × 64 grid**. It reports **75.48°C and 16,426 mm total routed wirelength** for the reconstructed original layout, compared with **75.47°C and 16,597 mm** for TAP's solution; its compact alternative reports 75.13°C and 23,794 mm. These are the authors' simulation results, not Huawei thermometry. The 0.01°C original-versus-TAP difference neither promises a large improvement nor supplies a physically calibrated acceptance margin. ([TAP paper, §IV-C, Fig. 6 and footnote 8](https://people.bu.edu/joshi/files/Ma_TAP-2.5D-DATE2021.pdf))

Our 50 mm substrate, explicit initial centres and historical grid-32 objective do not reproduce that original-layout experiment. The later software default of grid 16 is another configuration, not the setting of the executed headroom run. The paper's actual routing objective also differs from our weighted centre-distance proxy. Consequently, subtracting SentiLab's 116.09°C from the paper's 75.48°C would not measure model error, package improvement or engineering failure. Exact coordinates/orientations, layer and cooling settings, power allocation, solver/source identity, metric and routing assumptions must first match. ## Conclusion

The next release should commit to **a bounded question whose measurement and schedule are already credible**, with a named owner for the evidence chain from reference input to final claim. That changes the practical meaning of success: a reproducible negative or unresolved scientific answer can be a completed experiment, while a visually convincing improvement can still be an incomplete or mismatched one. We should allow evidence to tell us that the human reference is already adequate, that the model needs repair, or that an agent adds no useful benefit under the chosen budget.

Carter's first findings should remain frozen through the adversarial exchange. Any peer challenge should identify the exact disputed claim, supply source or measurement evidence, and state which conclusion or proposed gate changes. Corrections belong in a dated follow-on revision or response linked to this freeze, so the independent starting diagnosis, its uncertainty and our responsibility for the original choices remain reviewable.
