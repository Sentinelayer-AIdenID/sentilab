## 5. Frozen physics, geometry and mathematical contract

The following is a proposed contract to verify against the actual solver, not a claim that the existing implementation already uses these equations or conventions.

### 5.1 Coordinates and geometric legality

Store each chiplet with stable ID, lower-left coordinates (xLL, yLL), width w, height h, layer, orientation, power-scenario mapping and explicit units. Derive center coordinates only as:

    xC_i = xLL_i + w_i / 2
    yC_i = yLL_i + h_i / 2

Pin whether rotation is allowed and how dimensions/power maps transform. Use integer grid indices where practical; define physical-unit conversion and tolerance once. For interposer extent Lx × Ly and boundary clearance b_i:

    b_i <= xLL_i; xLL_i + w_i <= Lx - b_i
    b_i <= yLL_i; yLL_i + h_i <= Ly - b_i

No two physical footprints may overlap. Define a rectangular guard-band clearance g explicitly: expand each footprint by g/2 for a uniform guard band and require nonintersection, with a declared equality/touching convention. This is not universally equivalent to Euclidean clearance. Boundary clearance and pairwise gap are different quantities and must not be double-counted. Heterogeneous clearances require an explicit pairwise rule. Reject NaN, infinity, duplicate/missing IDs, negative dimensions, malformed units and coordinate/center mismatches before simulation.

### 5.2 Thermal task and constraints

Freeze chiplet dimensions, package/material/stack parameters, ambient/cooling boundaries, power scenarios S and chiplet-power allocation. The reference model may be a thermal network with steady-state relation G(T - Ta) = P or its transient counterpart C dT/dt + G(T - Ta) = P; inspect the actual implementation and document its assumptions. Do not retrofit an unverified PDE/network interpretation into a result label.

G1 must choose and version the eight-chiplet metric. The attachments proposed center readings; the independently checked current oracle supports a **proposed chip-layer grid-peak primary objective**, with center readings as diagnostics. This change is not approved merely because a reviewer recommended it. If adopted, assign new metric_id/evaluator_version, verify mapping/layer/resolution and use:

    J_grid(x) = max over s in S, max over active-chip-layer grid cells of Tcell_s(x)

The attachment's alternative remains explicitly identifiable:

    J(x) = max over s in S, max over i in {1,...,8} of Tcenter_i,s(x)

Lower the approved J is better, subject to all constraints. Export all scenarios, relevant fields and diagnostics. Grid peak is a sampled chip-layer maximum, not a certified physical junction peak. Current source parsing of chip-layer data must not be overwritten by the attachment's substrate-only claim; every field carries its actual layer/statistic. Existing six-chiplet grid-peak results, proposed eight-chiplet grid-peak results and historical eight-chiplet center results cannot be compared as the same metric. G1 approval and new protocol/version are required before switching the primary statistic.

Use centers for the explicitly approximate interconnect cost. Reconcile this proposed raw-sum form with the pinned current TAP weighted-mean implementation before freezing the schema:

    W(x) = sum over i<j of c_ij * (abs(xC_i-xC_j) + abs(yC_i-yC_j))
    W(x) <= W(x0) * (1 + alpha)   [if W(x0) > 0]

Freeze the nonnegative connection weights c_ij, alpha, reference starting layout x0 and units. Handle zero baseline by a separately frozen absolute cap. The reference is x0, not each newly accepted parent: otherwise repeated allowances can silently expand the total wire budget. This proxy is neither routed interconnect nor timing/power-delivery/manufacturing signoff.

Current TAP computes W_mean = W_sum / sum(c_ij). With a fixed nonzero connection matrix, ratios against the same baseline are equivalent; absolute thresholds and reported units/numbers are not interchangeable. Either retain the mean as an explicitly named metric or version the sum; a reviewer +1 must resolve this before comparison.

### 5.3 Verification, convergence and improvement margin

Reproduce saved solver/controller tests and baseline artifacts first. Add meaningful checks for power conservation, energy balance, symmetry, zero power, grid/unit transformations, malformed geometry, uniform-temperature limiting cases, solver failure and scenario completeness. Check passive linear-network heating monotonicity where that assumption applies and a slab/network case with delta_T = P * R_theta. Keep W, W/m2, W/m3, m, K/W and W/(m K) distinct; use absolute temperature if radiation is modeled. Compare selected cases against a separate analytic/simple numerical reference. A fresh rerun of the same binary establishes repeatability, not model-form correctness.

Measure sensitivity across at least three spatial resolutions, convergence tolerance and transient timestep when applicable; verify promising candidates on a finer grid. Specifically attack rasterization, center sampling, interpolation and grid-aliasing exploits. Record contact/interface resistance, material/boundary uncertainty and sensitivity separately from numerical repeatability. A finite scenario maximum is not a worst case over every physical workload; fixed-power steady-state results do not include dynamic workload or temperature-dependent leakage unless modeled. Freeze the acceptance margin before search. A conservative proposed rule is:

    J(board_current) - J(candidate) >= delta_min + u_num(board_current) + u_num(candidate)

Here delta_min is the declared practically meaningful improvement and u_num is a documented numerical-error estimate from convergence checks, not a fabricated statistical confidence bound. Model-form/physical uncertainty limits the claims even if this rule passes. Values for delta_min and tolerances remain TBD and require scientific +1. Candidate reproduction must meet the separately pinned reproducibility tolerance.

The current adapter sets converged=True after validating output. That establishes output validity under its checks, not numerical mesh/timestep convergence. Preserve or rename that status accurately and produce separate multi-resolution convergence evidence before relying on the uncertainty margin.

Lifecycle records proposed/validated/evaluated/accepted/rejected/failed and completed non-improving/tie/inconclusive outcomes. All fields are pure projections of append-only events so live and replay agree. Incompatible task/metric/evaluator/coordinate/scenario context must fail comparison rather than be silently coerced.

Represent sufficient support as an **OR of AND-sets**: supports = [[e1,e2],[e3]] means either the entire compatible current set e1 AND e2, or the entire set e3, suffices, while the claim's own mandatory checks must also pass. Formally, supported(c) = own_checks(c) AND OR_over_clauses(AND_over_e_in_clause current_and_compatible(e)). Withdrawal of e1 invalidates the first set but preserves a claim if e3 and the own checks remain valid. Reject cyclic self-proof in support edges; non-proof relation cycles do not manufacture support. Scientific validity and principal visibility/authorization are separate. Audience usability requires one entire clause whose every member is both current/compatible and authorized, plus own checks; do not combine partial fragments from different clauses. Viewing a record does not make it scientifically true.

Include trained_on edges from **every actual training input** through dataset manifests to adapters and benchmark provenance. Taint follows every withdrawn/revoked input actually consumed, regardless of whether an OR-support alternative keeps a related scientific claim valid. Withdrawal propagates eligibility/quarantine/taint; it does not erase historical observations or unlearn weights. A separately measured candidate can retain independent simulator support while a training-provenance claim remains tainted. Historical results remain recorded with the appropriate provenance warning and reevaluation policy.

An accepted update requires a fully materialized candidate bound to the immutable task/context, valid geometry/constraints, all scenarios, qualifying current-board objective, current supports and fresh reproduction. **A complete absolute candidate retains valid numerical evidence when the board advances**, provided its immutable task/metric/evaluator/scenarios match. Compare it atomically against the current accepted board using the frozen margin; on a compare-and-swap race, reread and retry the comparison without rerunning simulation or charging the same job again. Keep parent lineage/credit, but do not discard a valid result solely for an older known parent. Delta actions must first materialize against their exact declared parent; never silently rebase a delta onto the current board. Unknown parents or task-context changes remain errors. This preserves the current controller's known-parent/materialize/current-lane comparison behavior in the future durable service.

Completed non-improvement, ties and inconclusive outcomes are valid recorded science, not infrastructure failures.
