Thermal Interface Materials

Liquid Metal and Liquid Metal Pastes

Indium Corporation’s liquid metals are pure metals, or metal alloys, that remain liquid at room temperature, with melting points between 7.6°C and 17°C. These alloys are primarily gallium-based, often combined with elements like indium, tin, or zinc. All our liquid metals are RoHS-compliant, non-toxic, and can be transported globally in full compliance with shipping regulations.

Product Overview

Wets to Many Surfaces

Liquid metals wet to most metallic and non-metallic surfaces through two mechanisms—oxide pinning, a physical bond of an oxide to a relatively smooth surface, and reactive wetting, a metallurgical bond.

Liquid at Room Temperature

Gallium-based metals have excellent thermal conductivity, making them an ideal thermal interface material. As high-density, low viscosity materials, these substances form a thin, self-limiting oxide layer on their surface when exposed to air.

Higher Temperature, Higher Thermal Conductivity

Another interesting property of gallium-based liquid metals is that their thermal conductivity increases with temperature, meaning that the thermal performance of liquid metal TIMs improves as well.

Electrically Conductive

Liquid metals are electrically conductive and must be contained to prevent leakage or shorts in electronics. Indium Corporation’s patented multi-barrier system ensures compatibility, corrosion resistance, flexibility, and durability with gallium-based materials.

Indium Corporation’s Patented Multi-Barrier System for Liquid and Hybrid Liquid Metals

Features

High-Density, Low Viscosity

Liquid metals have viscosity similar to water, but a density that is six times greater.

Non-Toxic Alternative to Mercury

Gallium-based liquid metals have properties like mercury, but they are not toxic. They do not evaporate and cannot be inhaled.

Flexible and Stretchable Electronics

Thanks to their electrical conductivity, liquid metals and liquid metal pastes can be used in flexible and stretchable electronics, where they form electrical connections between components.

Excellent Thermal and Electrical Conductivity

Liquid metal is a pure metal or metal alloy that is liquid at or near room temperature and offers far superior thermal performance relative to non-metallic liquids. Our liquid metals for use in TIM0 and TIM1 thermal applications of CPU/GPUs remain liquid between 7.6 and 17°C (45.68–62.6°F).
